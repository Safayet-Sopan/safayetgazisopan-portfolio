BEYOND

Beyond Code

I'm a Vocalist

Music has always been a big part of who I am. Singing gives me a way to express emotions, tell stories, and connect with people in a way that words alone sometimes can't. I love covering songs from the artists and bands that inspire me, putting my own feeling into every performance, and constantly challenging myself to grow as a vocalist.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to perform at different live shows and events, each one bringing a completely different experience. There's something special about standing in front of a crowd, hearing everyone sing along, and feeling the energy build throughout a performance. Whether it's an intimate acoustic session or a loud stage with a full band, performing is where I feel most at home.

For me, being a vocalist isn't just about hitting the right notes. It's about emotion, presence, and connection — making someone feel something through a song and creating a moment they'll remember.

And a Guitarist

When I'm not singing, you'll probably find me with a guitar in my hands. Guitar gives me another way to understand and experience music — not just as someone performing a melody, but as someone who can build the foundation around it.

I enjoy learning songs, experimenting with different chords and riffs, and finding new ways to make familiar music feel like my own. Playing guitar has also helped me understand songwriting and arrangement from a completely different perspective, especially when working with other musicians.

Whether I'm sitting alone and figuring out a new progression, casually jamming with friends, accompanying my own vocals, or playing on stage with a band, the guitar has become an important part of how I create music.

Singing lets me express the song. Playing guitar lets me help build it.
